# ZIP-317: Proportional Transfer Fee Mechanism

**Status**: Standard  
**Official Specification**: [zips.z.cash/zip-0317](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0317)  
**Applicability to Nocturne**: Calculating network transaction fees for payout transactions and ensuring transactions are mined promptly without overpaying.

---

## 1. Fee Calculation Overview

ZIP 317 calculates fees proportionally based on "logical actions" to deter transaction stuffing:
$$\text{Fee} = \text{marginal\_fee} \times \max(\text{grace\_actions}, \text{logical\_actions})$$

* **Marginal Fee**: 5,000 zatoshis (0.00005 ZEC).
* **Grace Actions**: 2 (allows standard 2-in, 2-out transactions at base fee).
* **Base Conventional Fee**: **10,000 zatoshis** (0.0001 ZEC).

---

## 2. Rules for Payout Transactions in Nocturne

* For standard 1-to-1 or batched payouts, budget **10,000 zatoshis (0.0001 ZEC)** per output action.
* In parimutuel settlement:
  $$\text{Net Pot} = \text{Gross Pot} - (\text{Settlement Network Fees} + \text{Protocol Fee})$$
* Winners receive net pot proportional to their winning stake.
